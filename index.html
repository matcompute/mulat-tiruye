Current focus

BUC-6G is my PhD research line: what it takes to make connectivity intelligent, sustainable and everywhere.

BUC-6G

One intelligent fabric, from orbit to core.

6G has to connect everywhere: terrestrial and non-terrestrial access, the edge and the cloud. I study how AI-native control and zero-touch automation can run that whole stack as one system, with low latency, good energy efficiency and built-in security.
